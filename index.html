Piano Pathfinder

Find comfortable piano fingerings automatically. Type in a melody and Piano Pathfinder suggests a fingering that minimizes awkward stretches, unnecessary hand movement, and difficult finger crossings — then lets you see it on the keyboard, lock in the choices you prefer, and explore alternatives.

1 Enter a Melody

Type a right-hand melody using note names — C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5. You can use sharps (F#4), flats (Bb4), and spaces or commas between notes. Then click Optimize Fingering to generate a recommendation.

2 Keyboard View

The keyboard shows the suggested fingering for every note — 1 = thumb · 2 = index · 3 = middle · 4 = ring · 5 = pinky. Prefer a different finger? Click its number on a chip below to lock it in, and the app recalculates the rest of the fingering around your choice.

3 Why This Fingering?

Piano fingering is a balancing act between comfort, efficiency, and musical flow. The app weighs many possible finger combinations and chooses the one that takes the least overall effort — accounting for hand movement, finger stretches, thumb-under motions, crossings, and position changes. 6 How It Works

Think of it like trying every reasonable fingering and choosing the most comfortable one. Instead of guessing, Piano Pathfinder compares thousands of possibilities behind the scenes and picks the option that keeps your hand moving naturally across the keyboard.

Many ways are valid

The goal isn't the one “correct” fingering — plenty of passages can be played several ways. This gives you a strong starting point that follows common piano-technique principles.

You stay in control

Lock any finger you like and the rest re-adjusts around it, so the suggestion bends to the way you want to play the passage.
